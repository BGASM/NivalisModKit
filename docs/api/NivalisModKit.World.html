Places(bool)

Every place in the loaded scene the game's compass can point at, near or far (the compass itself only lists what's near the player): travel points, trains, boats and lifts with their destinations, greenhouses, shelters, and the player's apartments (others too with allApartments; every apartment door has a marker, rented or not). Two sources: scene transitions (doors, taxis, trains, boats, lifts) carry their compass type and a portal key naming where they go; apartment doors carry CompassMarker components. A full scene search: call it when a scene loads (it fills in over a few seconds), not every frame.
